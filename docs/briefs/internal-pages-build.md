STEP 02 — BUILD THE COMPLETE INTERNAL WEBSITE PAGES
NAJIA COMMUNITY — SAUDI ARABIC WEBSITE

IMPORTANT:
The Homepage has already been designed in Figma Make.

DO NOT redesign, replace, restart, or create a new visual direction for the Homepage.

Use the existing Homepage as the MASTER DESIGN REFERENCE for the entire website.

The goal of this step is to build all internal pages while extending the EXACT SAME design system, visual language, components, typography, colors, spacing, buttons, cards, icons, imagery style, and animation language.

The entire website must feel like ONE coherent product designed by the same design team.

==================================================
1. SAUDI WEBSITE — VERY IMPORTANT
==================================================

Najia is a Saudi Arabian community.

The website must clearly feel culturally appropriate for Saudi Arabia.

This is NOT a generic international website.

All people shown in imagery must be culturally appropriate for Saudi Arabia.

For female imagery:

- Use Saudi / Gulf women.
- Women should be modestly dressed.
- Prefer hijab and culturally appropriate clothing.
- Avoid revealing clothing.
- Avoid Western fashion styling that feels culturally inappropriate.
- Avoid unrealistic or stereotypical Middle Eastern imagery.
- Keep the photography premium, natural, realistic and emotionally authentic.

For family, community and healthcare scenes:

- Respect Saudi social and cultural values.
- Use appropriate gender interaction.
- Use culturally appropriate environments.
- Use Saudi / Gulf architectural and lifestyle cues where relevant.

The visual language should communicate:

Saudi
Arabic
Modern
Premium
Human
Respectful
Warm
Medical / healthcare aware
Community-focused

Do NOT overuse obvious Saudi symbols just to make it look Saudi.

The Saudi identity should feel natural and sophisticated.

==================================================
2. CULTURAL CONSISTENCY
==================================================

Review every existing visual element from the Homepage and internal pages.

If an image, illustration, icon, character, object, clothing style, environment or visual element conflicts with Saudi cultural context, replace it with an appropriate alternative.

- Unmodest female imagery
- Non-Arab cultural styling
- Inappropriate clothing
- Generic Western lifestyle scenes
- Generic hospital stock imagery
- Cultural stereotypes
- Inappropriate gestures or interactions

Use realistic Saudi / Gulf visual references where people are required.

23. TABLET — VERY IMPORTANT
==================================================

Tablet is a REAL layout target.

Do NOT treat Tablet as a smaller Desktop.

Do NOT turn Tablet navigation into scrolling through Homepage sections.

Tablet remains a MULTI-PAGE WEBSITE.

Example:

Tablet:
Click "المعرفة"
→ Navigate to /knowledge

NOT:

Tablet:
Click "المعرفة"
→ Scroll to Knowledge section on Homepage

Tablet layouts should intelligently rearrange content.

Examples:

2-column layouts
2×2 grids
Horizontal content sections
Reduced spacing
Readable typography
Touch-friendly controls

Preserve the full content hierarchy.

Do not remove important pages or sections simply because the screen is smaller.
